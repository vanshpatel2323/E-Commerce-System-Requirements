# Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to identify the key stakeholders involved in the E-Commerce System Requirements project and understand their roles, responsibilities, and interests.

## 2. Stakeholder Identification

| Stakeholder      | Role                      | Responsibilities                                                   | Interest                                  |
| ---------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Customer         | End User                  | Browse products, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries | Easy and reliable shopping                |
| Admin            | System Administrator      | Manage products, inventory, and customer orders                    | Accurate and efficient operations         |
| Business Owner   | Business Decision-Maker   | Define business objectives and monitor business performance        | Business growth and customer satisfaction |
| Business Analyst | Requirements Analyst      | Gather, analyze, and document business requirements                | Clear and complete requirements           |
| Development Team | System Developers         | Implement the approved system requirements                         | Clear technical specifications            |
| QA/Testing Team  | Quality Assurance         | Test system functionality and validate requirements                | Reliable system behavior                  |
| Payment Gateway  | External Service          | Process online payments and return payment status                  | Secure and accurate transactions          |
| Delivery Partner | External Service Provider | Deliver orders and update delivery status                          | Timely and accurate deliveries            |

## 3. Business Analyst Responsibilities

The Business Analyst acts as a bridge between business stakeholders and the technical team.

Key responsibilities include:

* Identifying relevant stakeholders.
* Gathering and analyzing business requirements.
* Documenting business and functional requirements.
* Preparing user stories and acceptance criteria.
* Clarifying requirements with developers and testers.
* Supporting User Acceptance Testing (UAT).
* Identifying gaps in existing business processes.

## 4. Stakeholder Communication

| Stakeholder        | Communication Purpose                            |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Business Owner     | Confirm business goals and priorities            |
| Customer           | Understand user needs and expectations           |
| Development Team   | Clarify functional requirements                  |
| QA/Testing Team    | Explain acceptance criteria and expected results |
| Admin              | Understand product and order management needs    |
| External Providers | Understand payment and delivery dependencies     |

## 5. Conclusion

Stakeholder identification helps ensure that business needs are understood, requirements are documented accurately, and the proposed e-commerce system meets stakeholder expectations.
