# Gap Analysis — E-Commerce System

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this gap analysis is to identify potential gaps between a basic manual shopping process and the proposed e-commerce system. It recommends improvements to make shopping more efficient, accurate, and convenient.

**Note:** This is an academic practice project. The current-state process represents an assumed scenario, not findings from a real business assessment.

## 2. Current State vs. Proposed State

| Business Area         | Current-State Challenge                                           | Proposed Solution                                              | Expected Benefit                                    |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Customer Registration | Customer information may be recorded manually.                    | Online registration and account management.                    | Easier account creation and information management. |
| Product Search        | Customers may need to browse products manually.                   | Search, categories, and product filters.                       | Faster product discovery.                           |
| Inventory Visibility  | Product availability may not be updated promptly.                 | Display product availability using inventory data.             | Better purchasing decisions.                        |
| Shopping Cart         | Product selection and price calculations may be handled manually. | Digital shopping cart with automatic total calculation.        | Fewer calculation errors.                           |
| Checkout              | Order details may be collected through manual communication.      | Structured online checkout form.                               | More consistent order information.                  |
| Payment               | Payment confirmation may require manual verification.             | Payment gateway integration and payment-status updates.        | Clearer payment tracking.                           |
| Order Confirmation    | Customers may not receive immediate confirmation.                 | Automated order confirmation after successful order placement. | Better customer communication.                      |
| Order Tracking        | Customers may need to contact support for updates.                | Online order-status tracking.                                  | Greater transparency.                               |
| Order Management      | Administrators may manage orders using separate records.          | Centralized order management interface.                        | Easier order administration.                        |
| Reporting             | Business performance information may require manual compilation.  | Reports for sales and orders.                                  | Improved business monitoring.                       |

## 3. Identified Gaps

### Gap 1: Limited Product Discovery

**Problem:** Customers may find it difficult to locate specific products.

**Recommendation:** Provide product search, categories, and filters.

### Gap 2: Inaccurate Order Totals

**Problem:** Manual calculations can lead to incorrect order amounts.

**Recommendation:** Automatically calculate item totals and the final order amount.

### Gap 3: Unclear Payment Status

**Problem:** Customers may not know whether their payment succeeded or failed.

**Recommendation:** Integrate payment-status handling and provide clear feedback.

### Gap 4: Limited Order Visibility

**Problem:** Customers may have difficulty checking order progress.

**Recommendation:** Provide an order history and order-status tracking feature.

### Gap 5: Manual Administrative Work

**Problem:** Administrators may spend unnecessary time managing products and orders.

**Recommendation:** Provide centralized product and order management.

## 5. Recommended Actions

1. Confirm business needs with relevant stakeholders.
2. Validate the proposed features and priorities.
3. Document approved requirements in the BRD and FRD.
4. Develop user stories and acceptance criteria.
5. Review the requirements with the development and QA teams.
6. Validate the implemented functionality through User Acceptance Testing (UAT).

## 6. Expected Outcome

The proposed system aims to reduce manual work, improve order accuracy, make product discovery easier, and provide better visibility into payment and order status.

All recommendations should be validated against actual stakeholder needs before implementation.
