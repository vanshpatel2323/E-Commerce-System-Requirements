# Agile Sprint Planning and Product Backlog

## 1. Purpose

This document describes a proposed Agile approach for delivering the E-Commerce System Requirements project in manageable increments.

The backlog and sprint plan are illustrative examples prepared for this academic Business Analyst portfolio project. They do not represent actual completed development sprints.

## 2. Agile Approach

The project uses an Agile-inspired approach based on iterative planning, prioritized requirements, regular reviews, and continuous feedback.

### Key Agile Activities

* **Backlog Refinement:** Review, clarify, and prioritize requirements and user stories.
* **Sprint Planning:** Select backlog items for the upcoming sprint.
* **Daily Stand-up:** Share progress, planned work, and blockers.
* **Sprint Review:** Demonstrate completed work and collect stakeholder feedback.
* **Sprint Retrospective:** Identify improvements for the next sprint.

## 5. Definition of Ready

A backlog item is ready for sprint planning when:

* The user story is clearly written.
* Business value and priority are understood.
* Acceptance criteria are documented.
* Dependencies and major risks are identified.
* The team has enough information to estimate the work.

## 6. Definition of Done

A backlog item may be considered done when:

* The agreed functionality has been implemented.
* Acceptance criteria have been verified.
* Relevant testing has been completed.
* Critical defects have been resolved or formally addressed.
* Documentation has been updated where necessary.
* The agreed team quality criteria have been met.

## 7. Business Analyst Responsibilities

The Business Analyst supports Agile delivery by:

1. Clarifying stakeholder needs and business requirements.
2. Writing and refining user stories.
3. Defining acceptance criteria.
4. Prioritizing backlog items with relevant stakeholders.
5. Answering requirement-related questions during development.
6. Supporting UAT and documenting feedback.
7. Helping assess the impact of changing requirements.

## 8. Expected Outcome

This plan demonstrates how business requirements can be organized into a prioritized backlog and proposed sprints to support iterative delivery, stakeholder feedback, and continuous improvement.
