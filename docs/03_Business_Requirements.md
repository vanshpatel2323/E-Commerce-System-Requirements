# Business Requirements

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the high-level business requirements for the proposed E-Commerce System. It identifies the business needs and expected capabilities required to provide a smooth online shopping experience.

## 2. Business Objectives

* Provide customers with a convenient online shopping experience.
* Simplify product discovery and order placement.
* Support secure online payment processing.
* Improve order tracking and order management.
* Help administrators manage products and inventory efficiently.
* Improve customer satisfaction and operational efficiency.

## 3. Business Requirements

| Requirement ID | Business Requirement                                                                     | Priority |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| BR-01          | The system shall allow customers to register and securely access their accounts.         | High     |
| BR-02          | The system shall allow customers to browse and search for products.                      | High     |
| BR-03          | The system shall allow customers to manage their shopping carts.                         | High     |
| BR-04          | The system shall support order placement and order confirmation.                         | High     |
| BR-05          | The system shall support online payment processing through an external payment provider. | High     |
| BR-06          | The system shall allow customers to view their order status.                             | High     |
| BR-07          | The system shall support cancellation of eligible orders.                                | Medium   |
| BR-08          | The system shall allow administrators to manage product information and inventory.       | High     |
| BR-09          | The system shall allow administrators to view and manage customer orders.                | High     |
| BR-10          | The system shall provide a reliable and user-friendly shopping experience.               | High     |
| BR-11          | The system shall provide customers with clear payment and order status information.      | High     |
| BR-12          | The system shall support business reporting on sales and orders.                         | Medium   |

## 4. Business Scope

### In Scope

* Customer account management
* Product discovery
* Shopping cart management
* Checkout and payment integration
* Order management and tracking
* Product and inventory administration
* Basic sales and order reporting

### Out of Scope

* Development of a payment gateway
* Physical delivery operations
* Warehouse automation
* Advanced AI-based product recommendations

## 5. Business Constraints and Assumptions

### Assumptions

* Customers have access to the internet.
* Administrators are authorized to manage products and orders.
* Payment processing is provided by an external payment service.
* Delivery status information is made available by the delivery partner.

### Constraints

* Payment processing depends on the external payment provider.
* Order tracking depends on timely status updates.
* Customer information must be handled securely.
* The system must follow applicable privacy and security requirements.

## 6. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful at the requirements-analysis stage when:

* Business requirements are documented clearly.
* Each requirement has a unique identifier and priority.
* Functional requirements can be linked to business requirements.
* User stories and acceptance criteria are defined for key workflows.
* UAT scenarios are prepared to validate the requirements.

## 7. Conclusion

These business requirements establish the foundation for detailed functional requirements, user stories, process analysis, and UAT planning.
